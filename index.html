Welcome to Child Desire Foundation

Child Desire Foundation is a registered non-profit organization based in Salima, Malawi, dedicated to supporting vulnerable children through education, nutrition, and care.

As a youth-led non-profit organization, we work directly with children at risk of dropping out of school due to poverty and lack of resources. Our programs provide learning materials, and mentorship to ensure every child has the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive.

We are a non-profit organization driven by transparency and community impact, guided by simple belief: Every child desires to be someone.
